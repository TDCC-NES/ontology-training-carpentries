## Status of this training

This training is actively being developed by the [TDCC-NES ontology engineers](https://tdcc.nl/nes/ontology-engineers/), Kathrin Füllenbach and Dani Metilli. If you teach it or have feedback on the materials, the maintainers would be happy to hear from you at [nes@tdcc.nl](mailto:nes@tdcc.nl), or via an [issue on the source repository](https://github.com/TDCC-NES/ontology-training/issues).

Three episodes are not yet complete, and are marked as "Under development" in the episode itself:

- [Maintain your ontology](../episodes/maintain-your-ontology.md) has not been written yet.
- [Reuse ontologies](../episodes/reuse-ontologies.md) is partially written: it covers assessing and reusing existing ontologies, and introduces `owl:imports`, but does not yet cover mapping approaches such as SHACL-AF rules.
- [Ontologies beyond RDF](../episodes/ontologies-beyond-rdf.md) has not been written yet.

When teaching this lesson before those episodes are complete, consider ending the workshop after [Publish your ontology](../episodes/publish-your-ontology.md), or treating the remaining episodes as optional further-reading pointers.

## Lesson structure

The lesson follows a single running example throughout: a fictional bicycle inventory (bicycle B001, its frame F001, and so on). Keeping this one example in view across episodes helps learners see how each new OWL or RDF construct builds on what came before.

"Logical modelling in OWL" is split across five episodes for pacing: [Logical modelling in OWL](../episodes/logical-modelling-in-owl.md) (overview), [Define classes logically](../episodes/define-classes-logically.md), [Property characteristics and identity](../episodes/property-characteristics-and-identity.md), [Check your ontology with reasoning](../episodes/check-your-ontology-with-reasoning.md), [Advanced OWL modelling](../episodes/advanced-owl-modelling.md) (optional), and the [OWL 2 cheat sheet](../episodes/owl-2-cheat-sheet.md) (a reference page learners can return to, rather than a page to teach in order). Depending on the time available, "Advanced OWL modelling" and the cheat sheet can be left for learners to read independently.

Several episodes depend on hands-on walkthroughs in Protégé, RMLMapper and pyLODE; see the [Setup](../learners/setup.md) page for what each episode needs, and confirm learners have the relevant software working before starting that episode.
